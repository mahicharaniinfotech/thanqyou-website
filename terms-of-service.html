3. How Earnings Work

Creators share in the advertising revenue earned on ThanQYou. Viewers are paid a set rate for the time they spend watching reels. Earnings are never paid for viewing or clicking ads.

Revenue Split

  • Creators: 40% — of each ad’s revenue, the creator of the first of the two reels shown before it earns 25% and the creator of the second earns 15%
  • Platform: 60% — viewer earnings are paid from this share

Viewer Earnings

  • Viewers earn ₹0.25 for each full 60 minutes of reels watched, counting up to 5 hours a day. There is no pay for part of an hour; minutes carry over until they reach 60. Your own reels don’t count
  • Viewing earnings are calculated at midnight and added to your wallet
  • Watch 100 hours or more in a month and you get a ₹200 bonus; watch 150 hours or more and you get ₹350 instead

Payout Schedule

  • Earnings are calculated daily and added to your wallet
  • Once your balance reaches ₹300 it is sent to your verified bank account automatically on the 5th of each month. There is no option to withdraw at other times or to pay by UPI
  • Below ₹300, your balance stays in your wallet
  • Payouts require KYC verification. Unverified users' earnings accrue and roll over until verification is complete — nothing is forfeited

Signup and Referral Bonuses

During our early user offer, which ends at the 25,000th signup, new users receive ₹5 on signing up, and you earn ₹3 for each friend who signs up with your code and stays signed up for 24 hours. Your first 3 reels of 15 seconds or more each day also earn ₹2, paid 24 hours after posting. KYC is not needed to earn these bonuses, but it is required before any payout.

Only post reels you made or have the right to post. Copied reels are removed and earn nothing, and 3 removed reels end your reel bonuses.

If your annual earnings exceed ₹50,000, providing a PAN card is mandatory under Indian tax law.
